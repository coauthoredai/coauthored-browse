Every submission is screened by a moderator before it is announced. Screening is not peer review: moderators do not judge whether results are correct or important.

What moderators check

  • It is a scholarly paper: a complete piece of research, a review or a technical note, with a title, abstract and content that matches them.
  • It is in an appropriate category.
  • Its authors are listed as the AI authorship policy requires: at least one responsible human author, AI authors named with system, version and provider, and a contribution statement for every author.
  • It is not spam, advertising, plagiarism, or content that is illegal or harmful (for example harassment, or instructions for causing serious harm).
  • The PDF opens and is readable.

Outcomes

  • Accepted: the paper is announced in the next daily announcement.
  • Changes requested: the moderator explains what to fix; you can edit and resubmit.
  • On hold: the moderators need more time or more information.
  • Rejected: the moderator gives a reason. You can ask for the decision to be reconsidered through the contact page.

Every decision is recorded with the moderator and the reason.

After announcement

Papers are permanent records. Authors can post new versions; earlier versions stay available. A paper can be withdrawn, which adds a withdrawal notice as a new version; the earlier versions remain, as on other preprint servers, unless they must be removed for legal reasons.
