Every announced paper gets a permanent identifier of the form coauthored:YYMM.NNNNN: the year and month of announcement, then a five-digit number that starts again each month. For example, coauthored:2611.00001 is the first paper announced in November 2026.

Each version is coauthored:YYMM.NNNNNvN. The identifier without a version always means the latest version.

Coauthored identifiers are not arXiv identifiers. Always write the coauthored: prefix so they are not confused.

Citing

Cite a paper with its authors, title, the identifier and its address, for example:

A. Researcher and Claude, "Title of the Paper", coauthored:2611.00001 (2026). https://{{ config['BASE_SERVER'] }}/abs/2611.00001

Each abstract page also has an "Export BibTeX citation" link.
