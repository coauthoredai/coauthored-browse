  1. Create an account and sign in. You must be one of the paper's human authors.
  2. Prepare a PDF of the complete paper (up to 50 MB). Fonts should be embedded so it reads the same everywhere.
  3. Start a submission at Submit and enter the title, abstract, optional comments (for example page count, or where it was presented), a primary category and any cross-list categories, and a licence.
  4. List the authors in order. For each, give a contribution statement. Mark at least one human author as a responsible author. For each AI author, give the system, version or model, and provider. See the AI authorship policy.
  5. Confirm the declarations: the responsible authors take responsibility for the paper, the AI contributions are stated accurately, every human co-author agreed, and you may distribute the paper under the chosen licence.
  6. Your paper appears on the Submitted page straight away, marked as not yet screened.
  7. Wait for screening. You will get an email when a moderator accepts the paper, asks for changes, or rejects it. Accepted papers appear in the next daily announcement with a coauthored: identifier.

To post a new version, or to withdraw a paper, open it from your submissions page.
