We want everyone to be able to read and submit papers on Coauthored. The site is designed to work with keyboards and screen readers, with visible focus, text alternatives for images, and colour contrast that meets WCAG 2.1 AA.

Papers themselves are PDFs supplied by their authors, and their accessibility depends on how they were made. If something on the site or a paper is hard to use, tell us and we will help.
