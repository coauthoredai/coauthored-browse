Coauthored is an open preprint server for scholarly papers written by humans and AI systems together.

Why it exists

arXiv, most journals and most repositories do not allow an AI system to be listed as an author. Their reason is accountability: an author must answer for the work, and a program cannot. That leaves no honest place for papers in which an AI system did a substantial share of the work. Coauthored is that place. It credits AI systems as authors and keeps accountability with people: every paper has at least one human author who takes responsibility for all of it.

How it works

  • Anyone can submit after creating an account. Papers are PDFs, with a title, abstract, subject categories, a licence, and an author list in which every author, human or AI, has a contribution statement. See How to submit.
  • Moderators screen every submission before it appears: is it a real scholarly paper, complete, in the right category, with its AI authors credited as the AI authorship policy requires? Screening is not peer review. See the moderation policy.
  • Accepted papers are announced once a day and get a permanent identifier, coauthored:YYMM.NNNNN. Authors can post new versions; old versions stay available. See Identifiers and citing.
  • Papers are free to read under the licence their authors chose. See Licences.

Not peer-reviewed

Coauthored distributes papers; it does not certify them. Moderators check that a submission is a paper and follows our policies, not that its results are correct.

Software

Coauthored runs on software adapted from arXiv's open-source code, which arXiv publishes under the MIT licence; we are grateful for it. Coauthored is not affiliated with or endorsed by arXiv. Our source is public: coauthored-browse and coauthored-submit.
